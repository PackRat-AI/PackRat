# Intro screens

A short, swipeable welcome that shows a first-time user what PackRat is for before they sign up or continue as a guest.

## The model in one paragraph

The first time someone opens PackRat, they see a handful of full-screen pages, each with its own colour, a large illustration, a title, two short paragraphs and a one-line tagline. They swipe or tap the arrow to move through them, with dots showing where they are. The last page hands them to the welcome screen, where they sign up, sign in or continue as a guest. The intro appears once per install and never again.

## The pages

1. **Welcome to PackRat.** What the app is: plan trips, pack smarter, and get outdoors with less guesswork.
2. **Packs.** Build packing lists from a gear catalog, see total and base weight, and know what you are carrying before you leave.
3. **Trips.** Put a trip on the calendar, attach a pack, and keep the plan in one place.
4. **Weather and guides.** Check the forecast for where you are going and read guides for the trail and the activity.
5. **Works anywhere.** Packs and trips are on the phone, so the plan is there with or without signal.

## Moving through the intro

- Swipe left or right, or tap the arrow, to change page.
- The dots show the current page and how many there are.
- On the last page the arrow becomes a button that opens the welcome screen.
- A Skip control on every page goes straight to the welcome screen.

## User controls

- Skip at any point.
- Go back to an earlier page by swiping right.
- The intro respects Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion; with Reduce Motion on, pages change without the slide animation.

## What this feature does not do

- It does not ask questions, collect preferences or personalise the app.
- It does not request permissions such as location or notifications.
- It does not show a paywall or mention PackRat Pro.
- It cannot be replayed from Settings.
- It does not appear for someone who reinstalls and signs straight back in from a restored session.
