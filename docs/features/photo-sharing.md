# Photo sharing

Lets people post photos from the trail to the community feed, bring the people
they hiked with into the post, talk about it, and send it anywhere else.

## The model in one paragraph

A user taps New Post, picks a few photos from the camera or their library,
writes a caption, and tags the people who were there. The post appears at the
top of the community feed, newest first. Anyone signed in can like it, save it
for later, and comment; comments can be replied to, liked, edited and deleted
by their author. Anyone tagged or mentioned hears about it with a notification,
and can take their name off a post they do not want to be on. Any post can
leave the app through the share sheet, as a link that opens the post or as a
photo carrying a small PackRat mark. Anything objectionable — a post, a
comment, a person — can be reported or blocked in two taps.

## Posting photos

A post holds up to ten photos and an optional caption of up to 500 characters.
A post needs at least one photo or some caption text; it cannot be empty.

Photos come from the camera or the photo library, and a post can mix both.
Before posting, the user sees the photos in order and can rearrange them or
remove any of them. Photos are uploaded at a size that looks sharp on a phone
screen; the original stays in the user's library untouched.

Location data embedded in a photo is removed before it leaves the device. A
post never reveals where a photo was taken unless the user says so in the
caption.

If the upload fails, the post is not lost. The composer stays open with the
photos and caption intact and offers to try again.

A user can delete their own post at any time. They can edit its caption
afterward; the photos are fixed once posted.

## Tagging and mentions

Typing `@` in a caption or a comment suggests people by name. Choosing one
links their name in the text and tags them on the post.

Tagged people get a notification saying who tagged them, and tapping it opens
the post. Each person is notified once per post, no matter how many times they
are mentioned in it.

A tagged person can remove their tag from any post. Their name stays as plain
text, but it no longer links to them and the post no longer appears among the
posts they are tagged in.

## The feed

The community feed shows posts from everyone, newest first. Each post shows its
author, when it was posted, its photos, its caption, and its like and comment
counts. A post with several photos swipes between them; tapping a photo opens
it full screen.

Liking is a single tap and can be undone. A user can save any post to come back
to it; saved posts are listed together and are visible only to the person who
saved them.

The feed refreshes when it is pulled down, and a newly published post appears
at the top for its author immediately.

## Comments

Every post has a comment thread. A comment can be replied to, and replies sit
directly under the comment they answer. Threads go one level deep: a reply to a
reply joins the same thread.

Comments can be liked. A commenter can edit their comment, which then shows as
edited, or delete it. The author of a post can delete any comment on it.

The author of a post is notified when someone comments on it, and a commenter
is notified when someone replies to them.

## Sharing outside PackRat

The share button on a post opens the system share sheet, so a post goes
wherever the user's phone can send it — messaging apps, social networks, or a
copied link.

Two things can be shared:

- **A link.** It opens the post in PackRat for anyone who has it, and as a web
  page showing the photos and caption for anyone who does not.
- **A photo.** The current photo, with a small PackRat mark in the corner.
  This is what goes to places that take images rather than links.

## Reporting and blocking

Any post or comment can be reported as spam, harassment, or inappropriate
content. A reported item disappears for the person who reported it straight
away and goes to the PackRat team for review; items the team removes disappear
for everyone.

The team works reports from a queue in the PackRat admin console. Each report
shows the reported post or comment as posted, who reported it and why, and how
many times it has been reported. A reviewer either dismisses the report, which
leaves the item up, or removes the item. A reviewer can also suspend an account
from posting and commenting, which hides everything it has posted until the
suspension is lifted.

Any user can be blocked from their post, their comment, or a notification. Once
blocked, neither person sees the other's posts or comments, and neither can tag
or mention the other.

## What the user controls

- **Who can tag them:** anyone, or nobody. Turning tagging off also stops
  mentions from linking to them.
- **Notifications:** tags and mentions, comments on their posts, and replies to
  their comments can each be turned off.
- **Their own content:** delete a post, edit its caption, edit or delete a
  comment, remove their tag from someone else's post.
- **Blocked people:** a list of everyone they have blocked, any of whom can be
  unblocked.

## What this feature does not do

- **No photo editing.** No filters, cropping or rotation inside PackRat; photos
  are posted as they are in the library.
- **No algorithmic feed.** Newest first is the only ordering. No following,
  ranking or "for you" view.
- **No live-updating threads.** Comments appear when a thread is opened or
  refreshed, not as others type them.
- **No reactions beyond like.** One kind of like, on posts and comments.
- **No platform-specific share buttons.** The system share sheet reaches every
  app the user has, so there is no row of Instagram, Facebook, X, WhatsApp and
  Telegram icons.
- **No tagging a spot within a photo.** Tags belong to the post, not to a
  position on an image.
- **No video.** Posts are photos and text.
